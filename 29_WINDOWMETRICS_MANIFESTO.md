# WindowMetrics Manifesto

People don't want calculators.

They want confidence.

They don't want formulas.

They want answers.

They don't want dozens of websites.

They want one place that helps them measure, compare, understand, plan, and decide.

Every feature should reduce uncertainty.

Every page should teach.

Every calculation should guide.

Every recommendation should lead to the next step.

WindowMetrics exists to make window-related decisions simple, accurate, and trustworthy.

If a feature does not help users make a better decision, it does not belong.
